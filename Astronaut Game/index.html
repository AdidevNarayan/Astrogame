1. NASA DONKI Space Weather API Checking Feed...

Endpoint: https://api.nasa.gov/DONKI/notifications?type=FLR

Simulation Use: Real solar flare observation notifications from the Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information (DONKI) at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. The game dynamically tracks solar storm classes (C, M, and X-class flares) to trigger in-game radiation alerts and power balance challenges.

Note: The simulation exclusively utilizes solar flare (FLR) radiation telemetry; coronal mass ejections (CMEs) have been omitted from the physics model for gameplay clarity.

2. NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)

Every post-sol scientific discovery references genuine research datasets hosted on osdr.nasa.gov:

  • OSD-658: Transcriptomic changes in Arabidopsis seedlings exposed to simulated Galactic Cosmic Rays at the NASA Space Radiation Laboratory.
  • OSD-343: Comprehensive multi-omics analysis of astronaut mitochondrial stress and spaceflight impact, validating NASA Twins Study data.
  • OSD-137: Rodent Research-3-CASIS: Mouse transcriptomic and histology data evaluating bone and muscle mass loss countermeasures in spaceflight.
  • OSD-522: Integrative transcriptomics and proteomics of spaceflight seedlings adapting to microgravity and ionizing radiation on the ISS.
  • OSD-48: Rodent Research-1 (RR1) NASA Validation Flight: Multi-omics and liver/tissue response baselines for long-duration human space missions.

3. NASA Engineering & Mission Architecture

  • NASA Marshall ECLSS: The 98% water recycling rate is achieved by NASA Marshall's regenerative Environmental Control and Life Support System (Urine Processor Assembly + Brine Processor Assembly) aboard the ISS (NASA ECLSS Overview).
  • NASA Glenn Research Center: Perovskite thin-film photovoltaic research tested in low Earth orbit aboard the ISS via the MISSE platform (NASA Glenn Solar Testing).
  • NASA VIPER TRIDENT Drill: The Regolith and Ice Drill for Exploring New Terrains (TRIDENT) developed by Honeybee Robotics and NASA Ames for subsurface volatile sampling (NASA VIPER Mission).
  • Artemis IV Architecture: Inspired by NASA's planned Lunar South Pole surface stay supported by the Lunar Gateway orbiting station (NASA Artemis Program).
